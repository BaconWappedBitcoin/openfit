OpenFit virtual trails: credits

Trees, bushes, cacti, rocks, mountains, crops and grass: made in code. Trees and grass wear the
project owner's own textures, created for the owner's game "wicklight" (bark and leaf sheets, grass
clump cards; owner-generated, used here with the owner's permission and released with OpenFit).
Files: flora/*.png. The tree, rock and grass techniques follow wicklight's art notes.

Ground textures from wicklight (owner-generated, same permission): ground/rock_*, ground/grass_*,
ground/dry_heather_*, ground/leaf_litter_*.

Skies and light, all Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
- sky/forest.*: "Forest Slope" by Andreas Mischok (https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_slope)
- sky/mountain.*: "Fouriesburg Mountain Midday" by Dimitrios Savva and Jarod Guest (https://polyhaven.com/a/fouriesburg_mountain_midday)
- sky/clear.*: "Citrus Orchard Road (Pure Sky)" by Dimitrios Savva and Jarod Guest (https://polyhaven.com/a/citrus_orchard_road_puresky)
- sky/sunset.*: "Belfast Sunset (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal, Dimitrios Savva and Jarod Guest (https://polyhaven.com/a/belfast_sunset_puresky)
- sky/overcast.*: "Kloofendal Overcast (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal (https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_overcast_puresky)
- sky/beach.*: "Secluded Beach" by Greg Zaal and Rico Cilliers (https://polyhaven.com/a/secluded_beach)
- sky/desert.*: "Goegap" by Greg Zaal (https://polyhaven.com/a/goegap)
The night sky, the sea, rain and snow are drawn in code.

Ground, Poly Haven, CC0 1.0:
- ground/forest_ground_04_*: "Forest Ground 04" by Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers (https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04)
- ground/sand_*: "Aerial Sand" by Rob Tuytel (https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_sand)

The Poly Haven files were taken from Patch (patch.gg), which selected the 1K versions, added a JPEG
background and wrote the credit into each file.

Engine: three.js r160 (https://threejs.org), MIT licence, bundled in assets/three/ (licence: assets/three/LICENSE).
