## 2. Equipment safety

- Always attach the treadmill's **safety key** (clip) to your clothing. Know where the console's **STOP**
  button is. Software, Bluetooth and tablets can fail; the safety key and STOP button are your real
  protection.
- Start at a slow speed. Never step onto a moving belt. Keep the area behind the treadmill clear.
- Keep children and pets away from the treadmill while it is on, and never leave it running unattended.
- Follow the manufacturer's manual, weight limits and maintenance instructions.
- OpenFit refuses to send speed or incline changes unless a workout it started is running, and disconnects
  when someone uses the treadmill by hand. These protections reduce risk; they do not remove it.

## 3. Health

- **OpenFit is not a medical device** and gives no medical advice. Heart-rate readings, zones, calorie and

- Talk to a doctor before starting an exercise programme, especially if you have a heart condition, high
  blood pressure, are pregnant, or have any other health concern.
- Stop immediately if you feel pain, faintness, dizziness or shortness of breath.
- Features that change speed from your heart rate ("Hold zone") depend on a sensor that can drop out or
  misread. Watch your own condition, not only the screen.
